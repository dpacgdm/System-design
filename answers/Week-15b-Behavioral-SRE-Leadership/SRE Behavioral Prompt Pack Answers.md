# Answer Key — SRE Behavioral Prompt Pack

## Timed set strategy
Pick 8 prompts across categories. 3.5 min each. Score each with rubric.

## Model outlines (selected)

### Prompt 1 — Incident led
STAR-L with T+0/T+5/T+15 personal actions; customer number; durable fix ≤2 weeks.

### Prompt 4 — Error budget decision
Show burn math; product tradeoff; what you allowed vs blocked; outcome.

### Prompt 6 — Disagreement on shipping
Incentives; data; concession; relationship check.

### Prompt 10 — Toil
Definition; hours before/after; proof at day 90.

### Prompt 14 — Ambiguity
Frame; reversible probe; kill criteria; what you learned.

### Prompt 21/22 — Too slow / too fast
Calibrated judgment; not personality brand.

### Prompt 23 — Failure you caused
Blunt; repair; prevention; no humiliation theater.

### Prompt 25 — Why this company
Map to how you already work (measurement, candor, impact) with one story pointer.

## Scoring
≥6/8 at ≥22 points = Staff pass for the pack session.
