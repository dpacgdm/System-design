# Pressure Communication Drills

### Foundation

Interviews fail when knowledge exists but **delivery collapses**. This module trains structure under interruption — for behavioral **and** system design narration.

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Deliver a 60-second, 3-minute, and 10-minute version of the same answer.  
2. Recover from interrupts without restarting from zero.  
3. Use check-ins (“Does that match what you want depth on?”).  
4. Score clarity separately from technical correctness.

---

## Wrong Mental Models

```text
1. "If I keep talking I'll cover it."
   Wrong. Unstructured talk burns clock and confidence.

2. "Interrupts mean I'm failing."
   Wrong. Interrupts are normal; recovery is the skill.

3. "Notes make me look weak."
   Wrong in practice; in interview, a 3-bullet skeleton is fine if glance-only.
```

---

## Core Drills

### Foundation — Pyramid

1. **Headline** (1 sentence): decision or outcome  
2. **Stakes** (1–2 sentences): numbers  
3. **Depth**: only what was asked  
4. **Check-in**

### Staff — Interrupt recovery protocol

When interrupted:

1. Stop mid-sentence cleanly.  
2. Answer the interrupt in ≤60s.  
3. Ask: “Want me to continue the timeline or stay on this?”  
4. Resume from a labeled beat (“Back to Action 2…”).

### Principal stretch — Hostile ambiguity

Interviewer: “That doesn’t sound Staff.”  
Response pattern: clarify bar → upgrade with tradeoff + metric + lesson — no defensiveness.

## Communication scorecard (use on every mock)

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|-----------|---|---|---|---|
| Structure | Lost | Occasional | Clear beats | Explicit roadmap |
| Brevity | Ramble | Soft | Tight | Tight + check-ins |
| Numbers | None | Late | Early | Early + capacity |
| Interrupt recovery | Collapse | Restart | Recover | Recover + redirect |
| Assumption checks | Never | Rare | Some | Habitual |

**Staff pass:** no dimension below 3.
