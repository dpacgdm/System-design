# SRE Behavioral Prompt Pack

### Foundation

Practice the **actual prompt shapes** FAANG uses for SRE/Staff loops. Answers must stay in STAR-L and hit company signals from the Culture Maps module.

---

## Learning Objectives

1. Recognize prompt class in ≤10 seconds.  
2. Select the right story card without rambling.  
3. Answer 20 core prompts at Staff bar under time.  
4. Handle nested follow-ups without breaking ownership.

---

## Prompt Catalog

### Reliability & incidents

1. Tell me about a production incident you led.  
2. Describe a time your initial root cause was wrong.  
3. How did you decide between mitigate-now vs find-root-cause?  
4. Tell me about a time you used an error budget to make a decision.  
5. Describe a cascading failure you diagnosed.

### Conflict & influence

6. Tell me about a disagreement with product/engineering about shipping.  
7. Describe influencing a team that didn’t report to you.  
8. Tell me about a time you had to escalate — and when you didn’t.  
9. Describe receiving hard feedback and what changed.

### Toil, quality, craft

10. Tell me about eliminating toil.  
11. Describe improving on-call / reducing pages.  
12. Tell me about a time you improved debugging / observability.  
13. Describe a risky change you made safer.

### Ambiguity & prioritization

14. Tell me about a vaguely defined problem.  
15. How do you prioritize when everything is SEV-flavored?  
16. Describe a time you were wrong about capacity.

### Leadership & people

17. Tell me about mentoring someone through a hard incident.  
18. Describe raising the reliability bar on a team.  
19. Tell me about hiring or leveling signal you care about (if applicable).  
20. Describe making a decision with incomplete data.

### Culture stress tests

21. Tell me about a time you were too slow.  
22. Tell me about a time you moved too fast.  
23. Describe a failure you caused.  
24. Tell me about something you’d do differently in your last role.  
25. Why SRE / why this company? (bridge to technical values, not flattery)

## Staff — Follow-up trees (expect these)

For any incident story:

- What were the alternatives?  
- What did you personally do in the first 15 minutes?  
- Who did you update and when?  
- What was the customer impact number?  
- What durable fix landed in ≤2 weeks / ≤1 quarter?  
- What would break if we applied your fix elsewhere?

For conflict stories:

- What was their incentive?  
- What did you concede?  
- How do you know the relationship is intact?
