# Mock Behavioral 01 — Reliability Ownership (30 min)

**Format:** Timed. Partner or self-record.  
**Level bar:** Staff SRE  
**Do not open answers until scored.**

## Schedule

| Min | Block |
|----:|-------|
| 0–2 | Warm-up: “Walk me through your reliability ownership in one minute.” |
| 2–12 | Deep story: incident you led |
| 12–20 | Nested follow-ups (diagnosis wrong?, alternatives?, customer impact?) |
| 20–27 | Conflict prompt: disagreed on shipping |
| 27–30 | Close: what you’d do differently |

## Interviewer pushes (use aggressively)

- “What did *you* do in minute 0–5?”  
- “Sounds like the team did that.”  
- “Give me the number.”  
- “Why not roll forward instead?”  

## Candidate rules

- STAR-L; ≤4 min per primary answer before follow-ups  
- Score with Behavioral Rubric + Communication scorecard
