# Behavioral Rubric — SRE Staff / Principal

## Scale (1–4 per dimension)

| Score | Meaning |
|------:|---------|
| 1 | Unacceptable |
| 2 | Below Staff bar |
| 3 | Meets Staff / hireable SRE behavioral |
| 4 | Principal-grade signal |

## Dimensions (8) — max 32

1. **Stakes & context** — impact numbers early  
2. **Ownership clarity** — I-decisions visible  
3. **Technical / systems accuracy** — mechanisms correct enough  
4. **Tradeoffs** — alternatives rejected with reason  
5. **Collaboration** — humans, incentives, updates  
6. **Results & evidence** — metrics or concrete outcomes  
7. **Learning / durability** — prevention, not vibes  
8. **Communication** — structure, brevity, interrupt recovery  

## Bands

| Total | Verdict |
|------:|---------|
| 8–15 | Not ready |
| 16–21 | Below bar — drill story bank |
| 22–26 | **Staff pass** |
| 27–32 | Principal signal |

## Automatic fails

- Blame without system incentives  
- Fabricated metrics  
- No personal agency  
- >6 minute monologue with no check-in after being asked a short question  

## How to use

1. Run timed answer.  
2. Self-score before keys.  
3. Partner score if possible.  
4. Only open model answers after scoring.
